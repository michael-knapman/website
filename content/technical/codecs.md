# Codecs

This is a compilation of lossy codecs used for media.

Ideal chain of codecs and containers for watching prerecorded videos:  
MKV container to hold the video and audio streams.  
AV1 video codec for the video stream.  
Opus audio codec for the audio stream.  
LC3 bluetooth codec.

Ideal image format:  
JXL

My most used nonideal image formats:  
HEIF  
JPEG
WEBP

What I am using right now when streaming YouTube (nonideal):  
AV1 or VP9 video codec for the video stream.  
Opus audio codec for the audio stream.  
APTX or AAC bluetooth codec.

What I am using right now for watching self-shot iPhone 16 videos (nonideal):  
MOV container to hold the video and audio streams.  
HEVC video codec for the video stream.  
AAC audio codec for the audio stream.  
APTX or AAC bluetooth codec.

What I am using right now for this website:  
MP3 for the audio. I do not have the original FLAC, and converting lossy to lossy is bad, so I will not be switching from MP3 to OPUS.  
WEBP due to good size compression, even from lossy-to-lossy.  
PNG for cursor files which are mandated to be PNG.

Future developments:  
Media containers will eventually be standardized to MKV.  
Video streams will eventually be standardized to AV1 and its successors such as AV2.  
Audio streams will eventually be standardized to Opus.  
Bluetooth audio streams will eventually be standardized to LC3 at 345 kbps.
Images will eventually be standardized to JXL.
All of the above are likely to occur due to the fact that they are all royalty free formats.
