# Loans

Disclaimer: I am not a financial advisor and this is not financial advice. Mentions of specific products are not endorsements. Products are only mentioned because they are representative of the market in 2026.

Cheap loans are an important tool, used to live a good life. Without cheap loans, the pursuit of happiness would be impossible for many, and for others, many housing and transportation options would remain out of reach.
Without student loans, student's can't go to school and realize their dreams.  
Without home loans and car loans, many would be forced to rent and walk against their will.
This article will highlight some of the cheapest and most useful loans for people to consider.

Definitions that will help distinguish the different types of loans:  
Callable loan: The lender can at any time demand instant repayment of the full loan amonunt. This is risky.
Non callable loan: The lender cannot ever demand instant repayment of the loan amount. This is safe.
